# Payment and booking

DEMO DATA: made-up company.

## How do I book an installation?

First we do a site assessment and send you a written quote. To book an installation date you pay a 50% deposit; our office then confirms the date with you by phone. The balance is paid on the day the installation is completed.

## How can I pay?

We accept EFT and card payments at our office. Our staff never collect cash at your home.

## Do you offer finance?

We do not offer finance ourselves. We can give you a written quote to take to your bank if you want to apply for a solar loan.

## Can I cancel?

If you cancel more than 7 days before your installation date, we refund your deposit minus an admin fee of R1 500. If you cancel within 7 days of the installation date, the cost of equipment already ordered for you may also be deducted.

## Can I change my installation date?

Yes, once, free of charge, if you tell us at least 3 working days before the date. Thabo Maluleke in our office handles date changes.
