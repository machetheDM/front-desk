# Installation FAQ

DEMO DATA: made-up company.

## How long does an installation take?

Most home packages (3kW and 5kW) are installed in one day. The Family 8kW hybrid takes one to two days. Business three-phase systems take two to four days.

## Do I need to be at home during the installation?

Yes. An adult (18 or older) must be at the property for the whole installation, and the power will be off for about two hours while we connect the inverter.

## Do I need municipal approval?

Backup-only systems (no solar panels) do not need approval. Grid-tied systems with solar panels must be registered with the municipality as small-scale embedded generation. We do the application for you; it usually takes 4 to 8 weeks, and the system can run in backup mode while you wait.

## Will I get a Certificate of Compliance?

Yes. Every installation includes an electrical Certificate of Compliance (COC) issued by our registered electrician.

## Which roofs can you install on?

We install panels on tile, IBR and corrugated iron roofs. Flat concrete roofs need a mounting frame, which is quoted after the site assessment.

## How long will the battery last during load-shedding?

With a 10kWh battery, a typical home running a fridge, lights, TV and WiFi (about 800W) has roughly 10 hours of power. A geyser, kettle or stove uses much more and shortens this a lot.

## Can the system run my geyser?

A 5kW system can heat the geyser in the day when the solar panels are producing, but not from the battery during load-shedding. We recommend a geyser timer so the geyser only heats in the middle of the day.

## Is there a monitoring app?

Yes. Every package includes a WiFi monitoring app that shows solar production, battery level and usage on your phone.

## Do you service systems?

Yes. An annual service costs R950 in Zone A, plus the zone travel fee in Zones B and C. Panel cleaning costs R45 per panel.

## Do you install systems bought elsewhere?

No. We only install equipment we supply, because we cannot give a warranty on equipment we did not choose.
