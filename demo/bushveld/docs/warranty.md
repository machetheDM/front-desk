# Warranty policy

DEMO DATA: made-up company and terms.

## What is covered and for how long

Hybrid inverters carry a 5-year manufacturer warranty.

Lithium batteries carry a 10-year manufacturer warranty or 6 000 charge cycles, whichever comes first.

Solar panels carry a 12-year product warranty and a 25-year performance warranty.

Our workmanship (the installation itself, cabling and mounting) is guaranteed for 2 years from the installation date.

## What the warranty does not cover

The warranty does not cover damage from lightning, flooding or fire; these are insurance matters. It does not cover systems that were moved, opened or changed by anyone other than Bushveld Solar & Electrical, or settings changed by the customer that let the battery discharge below the minimum level we set.

## How to make a warranty claim

Send us a WhatsApp with your invoice number and a photo of the inverter screen or the fault. Pieter van Wyk handles warranty claims. In Zone A a technician visits within 3 working days; in Zones B and C within 5 working days.

Warranty repairs and replacements are free. If the fault is not covered, we quote before doing any work.
