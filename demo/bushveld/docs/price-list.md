## Travel fees by zone

Zone A: Polokwane CBD, Bendor, Flora Park, Fauna Park, Westenburg, Ivy Park, Sterpark and Seshego. No travel fee.

Zone B: Mankweng, Lebowakgomo, Moletjie and Mokopane. Travel fee R850 per installation.

Zone C: Marble Hall, Groblersdal, Tzaneen and Louis Trichardt. Travel fee R1 900 per installation.

We do not install outside these zones at the moment.

## Pensioner discount

Pensioners (60 and older) get 5% off the labour portion of an installation. Proof of age is needed when you pay the deposit. There are no other discounts.
