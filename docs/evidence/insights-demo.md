# Owner insights on the demo tenant (synthetic data)

Generated by `python -m frontdesk_ml.analyses`; runs in CI on every pull request. Every input is
made up (Bushveld Solar & Electrical does not exist), so these numbers show the method, not a market.

## 1. Rates, with their uncertainty

- Answered from the documents: 133 of 212 (63%; 95% CI 56% to 69%)
- Of the messages that arrived after hours, answered at once: 105 of 161 (65%; 95% CI 58% to 72%)

Intervals are Wilson 95%. With a month of messages from one business, a 6-point-wide interval is
the honest precision; a single percentage would claim more than the data holds.

## 2. What the documents are missing, grouped into topics

39 replayed questions were handed over because no document answered them. UMAP (to 5
dimensions) then HDBSCAN groups them; each topic is named by class-based TF-IDF and shown with
its most central question.

| Topic (top words) | Questions | Most central example |
|---|---|---|
| backup, between, difference, hybrid | 14 | What's the difference in price between the 5kW backup and the 5kW hybrid? |
| install, musina, january, prices | 9 | Can you install in Musina? |
| battery, warranty, after, cover | 6 | Is my battery covered if it got damaged after load shedding? |
| clean, panels, solar, brand | 5 | do you clean solar panels |
| inverter, claim, error, shows | 3 | How do I claim warranty, my inverter shows an error |
| (not in any topic) | 2 | I bought an inverter online, can you install it for me? |

How good is the grouping? On the 150 labelled evaluation questions (16 intents):

| Method | Questions clustered | Adjusted Rand index | NMI |
|---|---|---|---|
| UMAP + HDBSCAN (used) | 93% | 0.231 | 0.551 |
| HDBSCAN without UMAP | 47% | 0.293 | |
| k-means, told the true number of intents | 100% | 0.124 | 0.445 |

Without UMAP, HDBSCAN gives up on about half the questions (calls them noise) and scores only the
easy half. The embeddings here are character n-grams, so topics follow wording; Cohere multilingual
embeddings on the Bedrock pipeline group by meaning (a seam until the dev account exists).

## 3. When messages will arrive next week

Twelve weeks of hourly counts; rolling-origin backtest over the last four weeks.

| Method | MAE per hour | MASE (below 1 beats last week) | Weekly total error | 80% interval coverage | coverage, busy hours |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| seasonal naive | 0.711 | 1.0 | 12% | 79% | 90% |
| four-week mean | 0.629 | 0.885 | 10% | 91% | 93% |
| level x profile | 0.61 | 0.857 | 10% | 94% | 97% |

Next week (level x profile): about **127 messages** (80% range 112 to 142); **67% arrive while the office is closed**. Busiest hours: Thu 18:00 (about 3.4), Fri 19:00 (about 3.3), Wed 19:00 (about 3.0).

Limits: the intervals over-cover (they are conservative) because hourly counts are small whole
numbers; load-shedding evenings are not predictable from the calendar and show up as misses.

## 4. Drift in what customers ask about

The weekly job compares this week's intent mix with the four weeks before. Simulated with the demo
mix: how often each rule calls a week "shifted" when nothing changed, and how often the rule used
catches a load-shedding week in which fault reports rise from under 1% of messages to 8% or 15%.

| Messages this week | First version (PSI >= 0.25): false alarms | Rule used: false alarms | Rule used: catches faults at 8% | at 15% |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 30 | 99% | 1.8% | 23% | 59% |
| 50 | 92% | 1.9% | 44% | 87% |
| 100 | 39% | 1.7% | 68% | 99% |
| 300 | 0% | 0.0% | 50% | 100% |

The first version (every intent kept, empty ones floored at a tiny epsilon) alarmed on noise most
weeks at small-business volume. The rule used merges intents under 5% into "other", smooths
counts, and needs PSI >= 0.25 *and* a chi-square p < 0.01 (`backend/frontdesk/drift.py`). It
trades some detection at very low volume for an alarm that means something when it fires.

## 5. Can one small business A/B test a prompt?

The pre-registered experiment `exp-2026-11-lead-with-answer` needs 906 customers per arm to see 80% -> 85%.
At the demo tenant's 31 answered customers a week:

```
exp-2026-11-lead-with-answer: resolved_without_staff from 0.80 by +0.05 (alpha 0.05, power 0.8)
needs 906 customers per arm, 1812 in total; at 31 answered customers a week that is 58 weeks.
  if the effect to detect were +0.05: 906 per arm, 58 weeks
  if the effect to detect were +0.10: 199 per arm, 13 weeks
  if the effect to detect were +0.15: 76 per arm, 5 weeks
```

And why the tool refuses to look early: in 4000 simulated A/A tests (no real difference), a fixed
horizon declared a false winner 4.4% of the time; checking 10 times
and stopping at the first p < 0.05 did so 18.9% of the time.

Conclusion for a pilot: test prompts offline on the evaluation set and the replay first; run a live A/B
only for large expected effects, or across several consenting clients.
