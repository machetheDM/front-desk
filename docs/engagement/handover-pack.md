# Handover pack (template)

What the client receives when the pilot becomes a service, or when it ends. Written for the
owner, not for an engineer. Fill the brackets; send it as a PDF with the attachments listed.

## 1. What you have

- An assistant on [WhatsApp number / website] that answers from your approved documents, says it
  is an assistant in its first message, and hands everything else to the people below.
- A staff inbox at [inbox URL]: the handoff queue with a three-line summary per conversation,
  the full conversation, reply, resolve, and the insights screen (owners only).

## 2. Who gets what

| Person | Handles | Inbox role |
|---|---|---|
| [name] | [topics] | [owner / agent] |

To add or remove a person, e-mail [operator contact]. Removing someone takes effect on their very
next click, even if they are signed in.

## 3. Keeping answers right (your part)

- **Documents are the assistant's only knowledge.** When a price, policy or opening time changes,
  send the new document; the old version stops being used the moment the new one is approved.
- **Review dates**: each document has one. The insights screen lists documents that are due.
- **"Questions the assistant could not answer"** on the insights screen: each one is a missing or
  badly worded FAQ entry. Fixing the top three each month is the cheapest improvement there is.

## 4. Your customers' rights (POPIA)

- **Deletion**: if a customer asks you to delete their information, an owner does it from the inbox;
  it reaches the messages and anything derived from them, and you receive a report.
- **Marketing**: only customers who send JOIN UPDATES receive offers; STOP ends all messages at once.
- **If something goes wrong**, we tell you immediately (we are your operator, section 21), with what
  you need to decide on notifying the Information Regulator and your customers (section 22).

## 6. If you want to stop

E-mail [operator contact]. You receive a complete export of your conversations, documents,
consents and handoffs with a list of file fingerprints, then everything personal is erased and
you receive a certificate saying what was erased and what remains (ids and timings only;
backups expire within 14 days).
